Become a donor

Register once. You decide when you are available and who can contact you.

How it works

Four steps from request to donation.

  1. SearchPick the blood group and city.
  2. RequestSend the patient and hospital details to a donor.
  3. Donor acceptsContact details are shared only after the donor says yes.
  4. DonateMeet at the hospital. Donors can mark themselves unavailable for the next 3 months.
BloodConnect is a student project (ITM University Gwalior, Ad. WebTech CSE0309). Donor entries shown here are sample data. For a real emergency, contact the nearest hospital or blood bank.
