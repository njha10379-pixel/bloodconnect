About BloodConnect

A web platform that connects blood donors with people who need blood, built as a project for Ad. WebTech (CSE0309) at ITM University Gwalior.

The problem

In an emergency, families often struggle to find a suitable donor in time. Donor details are scattered, availability is unclear, and requests get delayed.

Objectives

  • Search donors by blood group and approximate location
  • Let donors register and update their availability
  • Manage blood requests in one place
  • Protect donor privacy and consent

Future scope

  • Connect hospitals and blood banks
  • Send SMS or email alerts for urgent requests
BloodConnect is a student project (ITM University Gwalior, Ad. WebTech CSE0309). Donor entries shown here are sample data. For a real emergency, contact the nearest hospital or blood bank.
